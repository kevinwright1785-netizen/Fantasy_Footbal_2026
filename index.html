The target
What to aim for through pick 63
4 RB · 2 WR
Composition beats order. All 64 orderings of your first six picks were tested. The top five are the same 4 RB / 2 WR mix and land within 18 points of each other — about a point a week. Get the mix right; the sequence barely matters.
WR-heavy is the real mistake. Six WRs early scores 1135 against 1358 for the best path. That's a 13-point-per-week hole.
RRW works but spends your flexibility. It ranks 5th of 64 — if you follow it with RB, RB, WR. Wander after that and you drop 73 points.
