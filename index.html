What the algorithm actually builds through round 6
RB-heavy · QB by R5
What the tool does on a clean board: RB, RB, RB, WR, QB, then best available. Three backs early, first receiver around round 4, quarterback in round 5. That\u2019s the algorithm following its own recommendation \u2014 not a script imposed on top of it.
Composition beats order. The early RB lean is because the top-RB tier decays faster than the top-WR tier, so the scarce thing goes first. Exact order barely matters; getting roughly 3\u20134 RB and 2 WR before your QB does.
The QB is a round-5 pick, with one exception. If Josh Allen slides to round 3, take him \u2014 he\u2019s 45 points clear of the field. Otherwise the QB4\u2013QB11 band is nearly flat and round 5 is the natural spot. Don\u2019t reach earlier for a non-Allen QB, and don\u2019t wait past round 6.
